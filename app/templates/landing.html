Early warning for stored water

Stored water changes between tests. We make that visible.

A low-cost node sits on the lid of a tank, watches pH, TDS and turbidity around the clock, and tells the operator in plain language when something unusual happens, so they know when to look and when to send a sample to a lab.

What the node watches

Proxy signals, not a lab.

What it tells you

Acidity or alkalinity. A sudden shift can hint at runoff, a leak or something added to the tank.

What it cannot tell you

What caused the change, or whether anything harmful is present.

What it tells you

The overall amount of dissolved material. A rise can mean salts or minerals are entering the water.

What it cannot tell you

Which substances are dissolved. It cannot see individual contaminants.

What it tells you

Cloudiness from suspended particles: sediment stirred up, runoff, or material shed from the tank walls.

What it cannot tell you

Clear water is not a certificate. Many problems do not make water cloudy.

Temperature

Warm water changes how a tank behaves, and helps explain drifts in other readings.

ORP

A hint of how quickly a disinfectant is fading between top-ups.

Our promise

A smoke detector, not the fire investigation lab.

What it is

  • A continuous, low-cost change detector at the source
  • An answer to: has something unusual happened, and what should be done?

What it is not

  • Not a safety certifier: sensors measure proxies
  • Cannot see bacteria, lead, arsenic, pesticides or fluoride
We say“No unusual change detected”
Or“Unusual change detected, test recommended”
Never“The water is safe”
