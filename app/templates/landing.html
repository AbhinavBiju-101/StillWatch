What it is

  • A continuous, low-cost change detector at the source.
  • An answer to: has something unusual happened, and what should be done?

What it is not

  • Not a safety certifier. The sensors measure proxies.
  • It cannot see bacteria, lead, arsenic, pesticides or fluoride.
