pH calibration

The device always uploads raw millivolts. A calibration converts them to pH, and can be applied to earlier readings too. It applies from its start time until the next one begins.

  1. MeasurePut the probe in each buffer and wait about a minute.
  2. SaveEnter the raw millivolts for pH 4 and pH 7.
  3. ApplyRecompute stored pH from the raw values, if you want old readings corrected.
